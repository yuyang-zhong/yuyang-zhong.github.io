FAQ

Common questions.

We have no technical staff. Can you still help?

Yes — that's my typical client. Everything I build is documented and trained so your team can run it without an engineer on payroll. If something requires ongoing technical care you can't support, I won't build it.

How do you handle data privacy and AI governance?

Guardrails come first. Before any AI tool touches your workflows, we agree on what data it can access, how long data is retained, and which tools pass vetting. I've drafted AI governance, responsible-use, and data retention policies for real organizations — and I bring that same rigor to yours. Nothing ships unreviewed.

How long is an engagement?

An audit takes one to two weeks. Sprints run a few weeks to a couple of months depending on scope. Fractional work is ongoing, month to month.

What tools do you work in?

Airtable, Zapier, HubSpot and Salesforce, Google Workspace, Notion, Python and SQL, LLM platforms including Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, and agentic engineering tools like Claude Code, Antigravity, and Codex. If your stack is different, the audit will tell us fast.

What does the free call commit me to?

Nothing. You'll leave with at least one concrete next step, whether or not we work together.
